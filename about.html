Company overview

A clearer business story for the next version of your brand.

PT. Mandala Kriya Semesta and PT. Mandala Kriya Utama are presented as companies engaged in selling and distributing quality local and imported food and beverage products. The current company profile positions the group for hotels, restaurants, cafés, beach clubs, bakeries, and catering businesses.

PT. Mandala Kriya Utama is described as a distributor of high-quality products for hotels, restaurants, and catering, with company roots from 2006 and a connection to PT. Soejasch Bali. The broader site also mentions a head office in Denpasar with branch presence in Jakarta, Surabaya, Bandung, Yogyakarta, and other major cities on Java.
