About the group

A trusted distribution base for HoReCa buyers.

PT. Mandala Kriya Semesta and PT. Mandala Kriya Utama are positioned as food and beverage distribution companies serving Indonesia’s hospitality and foodservice sectors. The live company information highlights local and imported products for hotels, restaurants, cafés, beach clubs, bakeries, and catering businesses.

PT. Mandala Kriya Utama is also presented as evolving from PT. Soejasch Bali, with company roots dating back to 2006. The current website further states that the broader business is connected to a distribution network with head office operations in Denpasar and branch presence across major cities on Java.
